# Official external references

The repository does not redistribute official Cambridge PDFs or complete transcriptions. Consult the originals at their publishers:

- [9618 syllabus, 2027–2029, Version 2](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/721397-2027-2029-syllabus.pdf)
- [9618 Pseudocode Guide for Teachers, 2027–2029, Version 1](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/721401-2027-2029-pseudocode-guide.pdf)
- [Computer Science 9618 subject page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-computer-science-9618/)
- [Cambridge copyright permission guidance](https://help.cambridgeinternational.org/hc/en-gb/articles/115004418469-How-do-I-apply-for-permission-to-use-Cambridge-copyrighted-material)

Older commits and internal notes may refer to local `syllabus-9618.pdf`, `pseudocode_guide-9618.pdf` or their `.md` transcriptions. Those files are intentionally absent. Tests and builds do not depend on downloading them. An official download link is not a redistribution license.

Project rule interpretation is in `CAIE_RULES.md`, execution decisions outside the guide in `runtime-conventions.md`, and implementation interfaces in `LANGUAGE_SPEC.md`. The project is not officially affiliated with or authorized by Cambridge.
